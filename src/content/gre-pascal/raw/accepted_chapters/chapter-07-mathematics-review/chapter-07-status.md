# Chapter 7 Status

The scanned pages for Chapter 7 (pages 143–151) were inspected and all discernible content was extracted.  The chapter is organised into four main sections:

1. **7.1 Algebra and Arithmetic** – Basic number properties, divisibility, prime numbers, exponent rules, absolute‑value properties and special products.
2. **7.2 Geometry** – Definitions and formulas for angles, triangles (including Pythagorean triples and special triangles), quadrilaterals, polygons, circles and three‑dimensional solids.  A brief overview of coordinate geometry is also provided.
3. **7.3 Data Analysis** – Descriptive statistics (mean, median, mode, range and standard deviation), the normal distribution, counting techniques, permutations and combinations, and elementary probability rules.
4. **7.4 Measurement and Currency** – Conversion tables for U.S. customary units of length, area, weight and volume, along with U.S. currency denominations.

All major formulas and explanatory tables were transcribed into the raw extraction and concept notes.  Because some exponents and tiny text were faint in the scan, a few items could not be read with confidence; these have been recorded in **chapter-07-needs-review.md** for verification.
