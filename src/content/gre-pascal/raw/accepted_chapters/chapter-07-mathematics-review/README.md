# Chapter 7 – Mathematics Review

This directory collects all extracted content, notes and data for **Chapter 7** of the GRE Pascal book.  Chapter 7 provides a mathematics review covering algebra and arithmetic, geometry, data analysis and measurement and currency.  There are no practice questions in this chapter; instead it presents definitions, properties and formulas.

## Files

- **chapter-07-status.md** – Summary of the extraction status and overview of what was processed.
- **chapter-07-raw-extraction.md** – Raw notes taken directly from the scanned pages, organised by section and page.  Includes formulas, tables, descriptions and references to the original image files.
- **chapter-07-concept-notes.md** – Cleaned and organised conceptual notes summarising each topic in the chapter.
- **chapter-07-formula-sheet.md** – Condensed list of formulas grouped by subject (algebra, geometry, statistics and measurement) with brief explanations.
- **chapter-07-worked-examples.md** – Worked examples illustrating how to apply the formulas and concepts discussed in the chapter.
- **chapter-07-data.json** – Machine‑readable representation of the chapter’s sections, concepts, formulas and examples.
- **chapter-07-needs-review.md** – Items that were unclear in the scan or require further verification, flagged for follow‑up.

Use these files together to ensure that all mathematical content from Chapter 7 is captured and can be reviewed or integrated into downstream applications.
