# Chapter 9 Status

This status report summarises the processing of Chapter 9.  After a second review of the scanned pages, it became clear that the chapter contains **six** practice sets, most of which are further divided into two parts.  Each part contains between 12 and 15 questions.  During the allotted time, we fully transcribed and solved all questions from the first three practice sets (43 questions total).  The remaining sets (Practice Sets 4–6, 108 questions) have been identified and transcribed, but due to the sheer volume they have not yet been solved in detail.  These unsolved items are marked for review.

Those solutions are captured in the `chapter‑09‑full‑solutions.md` document and the corresponding entries in `chapter‑09‑data.json`.  After discovery of additional practice sets, we conducted a high‑level inventory of the remaining 108 questions (Practice Sets 4–6) and transcribed their prompts in the raw‑extraction file.  However, detailed step‑by‑step solutions and data‑schema entries have not yet been prepared for these questions.

## Items Flagged for Review

The following issues require follow‑up when revisiting the unsolved practice sets or ambiguous scans:

1. **Practice Set 2 – Part 1, Question 5:** The multiple‑choice options for the equation \(8^m = 16^t\) were blurred.  Although the algebra shows \(m = \frac{4t}{3}\), confirm which printed option corresponds to this relationship.
2. **Practice Set 2 – Part 1, Question 3:** The right‑triangle diagram included tiny angle markings.  The solution assumes two right angles and an obtuse angle of 110°.  Verify these markings in a clearer copy.
3. **Practice Set 1 – Part 2, Question 4:** Wording concerning the number of students enrolled in exactly one course versus at least one course needs verification.  The solution equates the counts but the phrase may refer to “at least one course.”
4. **Practice Sets 4–6:** All questions in these sets are currently unsolved.  Each will need a full solution and data‑schema entry.  Particular attention should be paid to:
   * Blur or faint text in diagrams (e.g., geometry diagrams and probability tables) which may affect interpretation.
   * Consistency of question numbering and mapping between raw‑extraction and data files.

Until these issues are addressed, the Chapter 9 package should be considered a draft for the later practice sets.  The raw‑extraction document provides the necessary text to begin crafting solutions.
