## Q61

**Statement:** The contestant proposed that people simply “love one another,” a suggestion that, while appealing, was rather \_\_\_.  
**Correct answer:** **facile**.

**Explanation:** The recommendation to solve complex social problems by “just loving each other” is simplistic.  *Facile* describes something achieved with little effort or depth; Merriam‑Webster notes that *facile* answers can be superficial【545566760691498†L91-L100】, and Dictionary.com stresses that it implies a lack of genuine understanding【40969201082781†L30-L67】.  It captures the idea of a glib, oversimplified solution.  Other options either praise complexity (*sophisticated*), emphasise novelty (*innovative*), or describe moral qualities unrelated to simplicity.

| Option | Meaning & rationale | Why wrong |
|---|---|---|
| **facile** | Easily achieved or superficial【545566760691498†L91-L100】【40969201082781†L30-L67】. | Correct: the proposal is simplistic.
| **sophisticated** | Cultivated or refined【357659134674232†L89-L100】. | Would praise complexity; opposite of “simplistic.”
| **profound** | Deep or insightful. | The suggestion is shallow, not profound.
| **novel** | New or original. | The idea is cliché, not novel.
| **subversive** | Undermining established systems. | Loving one another is not subversive.

## Q62

**Explanation:** Community‑based participatory research entails shared decision‑making and collaboration.  The sentence contrasts “circumvents” (to bypass) with another word meaning “serves as an example of.”  *Exemplifies* (to illustrate or serve as an example) completes the contrast.  Other options like “undermines,” “detracts,” or “dispels” would imply the project weakens or destroys participatory research, contrary to the supportive context.

| Option | Meaning | Why wrong |
|---|---|---|

| **subverts** | Undermines or overturns. | Opposite of supporting; would match “circumvents.”
| **obscures** | Makes unclear. | Does not fit the idea of modelling.
| **detracts** | Takes away from. | Would imply harm.
