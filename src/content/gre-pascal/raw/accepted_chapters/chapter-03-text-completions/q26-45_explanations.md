## Q26

**Correct answer:** *sophisticated*.

**Why this answer fits:**  The sentence contrasts the “alluring excitement of Paris” with the “simple and picturesque charms” of rural America.  The blank modifies **excitement** and must convey the cosmopolitan, cultured appeal of Paris.  *Sophisticated* means “having a refined knowledge of the ways of the world”【357659134674232†L89-L100】, which aptly characterizes Paris.  None of the other options capture this refined allure.

**Why the other options are wrong:**

| Option | Meaning | Why it doesn’t fit |
|---|---|---|
| *Lascivious* | Showing or expressing sexual desire【680919049000652†L82-L84】 | Too sexualized and negative to describe the allure of a city. |
| *Foreign* | From another country; unfamiliar | Describes otherness but not sophistication or culture. |
| *Alien* | Strange, unfamiliar | Implies something hostile or inhuman, not the charm of Paris. |
| *Extraneous* | Irrelevant or unrelated | Means unnecessary; does not modify excitement meaningfully. |

## Q27

**Correct answer:** *lionize*.

**Why this answer fits:**  The sentence says people have a “widespread tendency” to celebrate successful retired leaders.  *To lionize* means “to treat someone as an object of great interest or importance”【431014272364933†L85-L87】, matching the idea of venerating heroes.  

**Why the other options are wrong:**

| Option | Meaning | Why it doesn’t fit |
|---|---|---|
| *Castigate* | To subject to severe punishment or criticism【52621951469038†L88-L93】 | Opposite of praise; the sentence describes admiration, not scolding. |
| *Remember* | To recall or commemorate | Merely recalling someone does not convey the hero worship implied. |
| *Appreciate* | To recognize the worth of something | Softer than the idolatry described. |
| *Sneer* | To show contempt or scorn | Implies mocking, not honoring. |

## Q28

**Correct answers:** *(i) cited, (ii) correct*.

**Why these answers fit:**  People often *cite* the short life expectancy of ancient humans to argue that the world continually improves.  To *cite* something is to mention it as proof or support.  In the second clause, we must adjust the numbers for infant mortality; *correct* means to adjust or make right.  After correcting for the high death rate of babies, the life expectancy of those who survived birth is nearly the same as today.  

**Why the other options are wrong:**

| Blank (i) option | Meaning | Reason for rejection |
|---|---|---|
| *Disregarded* | Ignored or paid no attention | The statistic is used as evidence, not ignored. |
| *Embodied* | Personified; gave tangible form to | Does not fit the idea of citing a statistic. |
|  |  |  |
| Blank (ii) option | Meaning | Reason for rejection |
|---|---|---|
| *Prepare* | To make ready | “Prepare for the fact” is grammatically awkward in this context. |
| *Read* | To interpret as written | One does not “read for” infant mortality rates here. |

## Q29

**Why these answers fit:**  The fashion doyenne’s remark is witty yet biting.  *Amusing* means entertaining or funny, capturing the humor.  *Caustic* describes sharp, cutting criticism; it fits her “acerbic wit.”  The combination accurately reflects her simultaneously humorous and acerbic style.

## Q30

**Correct answers:** *(i) guards, (ii) fecund, (iii) progeny*.

**Why these answers fit:**  Soldier ants perform security and labor duties, so they act as both **guards** and laborers.  The queen must be highly fertile; *fecund* means “fruitful in offspring or vegetation”【470903558279712†L90-L100】.  The queen produces **progeny**, i.e., descendants or offspring【477490883810385†L95-L100】, to keep the colony populated.

**Why the other options are wrong:**

| Option | Meaning | Why it doesn’t fit |
|---|---|---|
| *Sages* | Wise or learned persons | Ants are not wise; they are workers and guards. |
| *Sycophants* | Flatterers or persons who seek favor | Irrelevant to ant roles. |
| *Efficacious* | Effective; producing the desired effect | Describes results, not fertility. |
| *Imperious* | Arrogant or domineering | The queen’s fertility, not her temperament, is relevant. |
| *Forebear* / *Ancestor* | Ancestors; persons who came before | The queen produces descendants, not ancestors. |

## Q31

**Correct answer:** *preposterous*.

**Why this answer fits:**  The argument likening home banking to flying cars now seems absurd.  *Preposterous* means “contrary to nature, reason, or common sense; absurd”【780125078476421†L84-L87】.  Home banking is common, so the earlier prediction appears ridiculous.

**Why the other options are wrong:**

| Option | Meaning | Why it doesn’t fit |
|---|---|---|
| *Prescient* | Showing knowledge of events before they happen | The argument was not visionary; it was wrong. |
| *Visionary* | Having foresight or imagination | Also suggests accurate prediction, which this was not. |
| *Realistic* | Representing what is real or possible | The argument clearly underestimated reality. |
| *Pithy* | Concise and forcefully expressive | The issue is the argument’s accuracy, not its brevity. |

## Q32

**Correct answer:** *lackadaisical*.

**Why this answer fits:**  Each generation accuses the next of being lazy.  *Lackadaisical* means “lacking life, spirit, or zest; languid”【88245751530472†L87-L92】, which captures laziness.  

**Why the other options are wrong:**

| Option | Meaning | Why it doesn’t fit |
|---|---|---|

| *Obstinate* | Stubbornly refusing to change | Relates to stubbornness, not indolence. |
| *Savvy* | Having practical knowledge or shrewdness | Positive; opposite of being criticized. |
| *Riveting* | Holding the attention completely; fascinating | Also positive and unrelated to laziness. |

## Q36

## Q37

**Correct answer:** *aesthetics*.

**Why this answer fits:**  Architects must consider beauty as well as function.  *Aesthetics* refers to the appreciation of beauty and artistic taste.  Clients often choose designs based on appearance, so aesthetics is a critical factor.  Options such as *exuberance* (overflowing energy), *utility* (usefulness), *price*, or *profundity* (depth of thought) do not capture the concept of visual appeal.

## Q38

| *Deprecate* | To express disapproval of | Suggests he disparaged his success; the context implies modesty rather than criticism. |
| *Belittle* | To make something seem less important | Again implies he downplayed rather than simply refrained from boasting. |

## Q40

He does not let political disputes **obscure** his description—*obscure* means to make difficult to understand.  He points out the **superficiality of our theories**, emphasizing that our theories are shallow and incomplete.  Together, these choices highlight his insistence on clarity and honesty in presenting scientific knowledge.

## Q41

**Correct answer:** *imperceptibly*.

**Why this answer fits:**  The land merges into the sea ice so slowly that one does not realize when crossing onto hidden water.  *Imperceptibly* means in a way that cannot be perceived by the senses; it suits a gradual, undetectable transition.  Alternatives such as *permanently*, *irregularly*, *precariously*, or *relentlessly* fail to capture the idea of a gradual, imperceptible change.

## Q42

**Correct answer:** *unguided*.

**Why this answer fits:**  The sentence says a haphazard investigation may yield facts, but normally discoveries come from focused research.  *Unguided* means lacking direction.  Other options (such as *timely*, *consistent*, *uncomplicated*, or *subjective*) describe different qualities but not the absence of direction.

## Q43

**Correct answers:** *(i) surprising, (ii) justifies*.

**Why these answers fit:**  It seems surprising that portraits—pictures of particular individuals—are displayed in high art museums.  However, the artistic skill with which they are painted **justifies** their presence.  Other options either lessen the sense of surprise (*understandable*) or fail to capture the rationale (*challenges* or *changes*).

## Q44

**Correct answers:** *(i) activism, (ii) indifferent to*.

**Why these answers fit:**  Simpson later became an activist; *activism* refers to vigorous action for political change.  Yet during his college years he was largely **indifferent to** politics.  Options like *apathy* or *affability* do not match the later transformation, and being *devoted to* or *shaped by* politics contradicts his earlier disinterest.

## Q45

**Correct answers:** *(i) fathomed, (ii) static, (iii) dearth*.

**Why these answers fit:**  To uncover the real meaning of statistics, one must **fathom** them—*fathom* means to understand thoroughly.  The population and meat consumption remained **static**, that is, unchanged.  Meanwhile, most people suffered from a **dearth** (scarcity) of not only meat but of food products; the growing gap between rich and poor meant that scarcity intensified.  Other choices such as *calculated* or *designed*, *plastic* or *demographic*, and *deceleration* or *surfeit* do not capture the intended sense of understanding, constancy, and scarcity.
